02 / Same entries, different exits

Every exit variation

Each standalone replay starts at $5,000 with the same approved controller. Rule definitions are unchanged; different exits can change recovery state, equity, admission and subsequent trade counts. Standalone profits cannot be added to form a portfolio.

Price-distance targets are gross R. “Winning net R” divides each positive trade's net P&L by its original all-in planned risk, then averages those ratios. It differs from average dollar win ÷ average dollar loss. Partial legs count as one economic trade.

Exit definitions, holding deadlines and statistical details

    All variants retain natural initial stops and original holding deadlines. Trailing and structure levels never widen a stop. Ratchets use preceding observed quotes or completed candles only. Target-limit fills are capped at the target before slippage; severe exits use an observed quote after a one-second delay. Losses can exceed planned risk. No missing-path loss is deleted.

    Sharpe uses weekday daily cash returns, 252-day scaling and zero risk-free rate; Sortino uses zero minimum acceptable return and downside RMS over all weekdays. Flat weekdays are included; these are descriptive ratios, not audited live performance. Annualized return is geometric extrapolation over the stated calendar interval. Weekly block-bootstrap intervals describe development uncertainty and do not correct for selection across all variants.
