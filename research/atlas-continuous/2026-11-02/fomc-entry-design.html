Scheduled FOMC entry design

DESIGN ONLY · NOT ACTIVATED · NO FINANCIAL OBSERVATIONS SCORED

One distinct research hypothesis: exclude a new NAS15 admission from ten minutes before to twenty minutes after an agency-scheduled FOMC statement, using only calendar versions actually available before that admission. The interval includes its starting instant and excludes its ending instant. Cancel that signal; do not delay it into a later fill. SPX remains unchanged.

This is an inference motivated by event research, not a published profitable trading rule. It can miss profitable news-driven entries. The window does not encompass a separate press conference, unscheduled announcements, or the risk of an already open position.
